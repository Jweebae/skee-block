Part one

Client account

Opened from the Account or Sign in button in the website header. One window holds every account screen.

Create an account

Who: a new clientStarts from: Account → Create account
Live
  1. Open the account window
    • Tap Sign in in the header (or the menu on a phone).
    • Choose the Create account tab. It explains the two steps: confirm the email with Google, then choose a name and password.
  2. Confirm the email with Google
    • Tap Continue with Google and pick a Google account.
    • Google shares only the name, email address and profile picture link.
    • Google sends the client back to 3kee.site; the account window reopens by itself.
  3. Finish the account (“Almost done.”)
    • First and last name, filled in from Google and editable (up to 60 characters each).
    • Password and confirm password, at least 10 characters.
    • Tick “I have read and accept the privacy notice” (opens in a new tab). The account cannot be created without it.
    • Tap Create my account.
  4. Account ready
    • The window shows “Hello, first name.”
    • The welcome email is sent once to this account’s own address.
    • From now on the client can sign in with Google or with email and password.
Emails
Welcome to 3kee
If something goes wrong
  • Not now closes the window. The account exists but is unfinished; the finish form appears again at the next sign-in.
  • If sign-ups are closed in Supabase, Google returns the client with “New accounts are not open yet.”
  • A client who first tries Sign in with a new email and then uses “Confirm with Google” still lands on the finish form, never on “Choose a new password”.

Sign in

Who: a client with an accountStarts from: Account → Sign in
Live

    • or Continue with Google.
  2. Signed in

Forgot password

Who: a client who cannot sign inStarts from: the panel that appears after a failed sign-in
Live
  1. Prove the email is yours, one of two ways
    • Confirm with Google: Google asks for its login again, which proves ownership.
    • Email me a code: enter the email first; the site always answers “If this email has an account, a 6-digit code is on its way” so nobody can test which emails have accounts.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code (email route only)
    • Type the code from the email “Your 3kee code”. It works once and expires in 60 minutes.
    • Send a new code unlocks after a 60-second countdown; use the newest code.
    • Back returns to the sign-in form; Confirm with Google stays available.
  3. Choose a new password
    • New password and confirm, at least 10 characters → Save password.
    • Cancel keeps the old password and leaves the client signed in.
  4. Done
    • “Your password has been updated.” The client is signed in.
    • A security email confirms the change.

If something goes wrong
  • Wrong or expired code: “That code is not right or has expired.” Ask for a new one or use Google.
  • Too many requests: “Please wait a minute.”
  • No email: check spam; the account emails are sent through the studio’s mail settings in Supabase, which are being repaired on 25 October (codes were not arriving).

Delete account

Who: a signed-in clientWould start from: Account → Delete my account
Planned
  1. Ask to delete
    • Account → Delete my account. A warning lists what is removed (name, email, booking history) and that it cannot be undone.

  3. Deleted
    • The client is signed out; signing up again later creates a new, empty account.

Part two

Booking the chair

The four booking screens exist today as a preview. Saving real appointments, the owner’s answer, reminders and reviews are agreed requirements that are not built yet.

Book the chair

Who: any visitor or clientStarts from: Book the chair, or a service card on the homepage
Preview
  1. Service
    • Choose one cut. Each card shows its duration; every base cut is 800 DA.
    • Changing the cut clears the chosen date, time and cut preferences.
  2. Add-ons (optional)
    • Tick an add-on (for example a protein hair treatment), then choose exactly one of its types.
    • Each type adds its own price and minutes. Only add-ons raise the total.
    • Continue is blocked until every ticked add-on has a type, or is unticked.
  3. Date & time
    • Dates for the next two weeks; closed days and days where the full service cannot fit are greyed out.
    • Times are grouped Morning and Afternoon, follow the saved working hours and breaks, and are in M’Sila time (UTC+1).
  4. Review
    • Summary of cut, add-ons, date, time, total minutes and total price, with “Change date & time”.
    • Optional cut preferences (from the owner’s list, no extra fee) and notes for Skee (up to 1,000 characters).
    • Preferred reminder channel: WhatsApp or Email.
    • The studio location stays hidden until a real appointment is confirmed.
  5. Send the request
    • Today (preview): a “Preview complete” screen says no appointment was made and links to WhatsApp to arrange the visit.
    • Planned: the client signs in or creates an account if needed and gives a WhatsApp number (saved for next time). The request is saved as Requested with a code such as SKB-4827, the slot is held so nobody else can take it, the client gets “We received your request”, and every owner gets an alert.
    • Times already held by other requests or appointments no longer appear in step 3. Bookings need at least one hour’s notice.
    • A client can hold at most two upcoming requests or appointments at once.
    • The client is told replies usually come within 10 minutes.
Emails (planned)
We received your requestNew booking request → owner Proposed
If something goes wrong
  • No times fit in two weeks: “Try fewer extras or contact Skee.”
  • Planned: if someone else takes the slot during review, the client is asked to pick another time before sending.

Client dashboard

Who: a signed-in clientStarts from: Account (design board 05 “My account”)
Planned
  1. Welcome
    • “Salam, first name.” with “client since month year”.
    • Visits (completed appointments) and Usual (the cut booked most often).
    • Book again starts a booking with the usual cut.
  2. Upcoming appointment card
    • Code, cut, date, time, minutes, “with Skee”, and status (under review or confirmed).
    • Reschedule: pick a new time; the old one is released and the new one goes back to review.
    • Cancel: allowed online until 3 hours before; after that the card points to WhatsApp.
    • WhatsApp: opens a chat with Skee, message prefilled with the booking code.
    • Reminder status: confirmation sent, appointment-day reminder.
    • View location: unlocked only once the appointment is confirmed.
  3. History
    • Tabs: Completed, Cancelled (including declined and missed), Deleted, each with a count.
    • Each row: date, time, cut, price, and the review state (“Not reviewed yet”, “Review submitted, awaiting approval”, “Review published on the site”).
    • Review on a completed visit that has no review yet.
    • Remove hides a row from the client’s history; it can be restored from Deleted. The owner still sees it.
  4. Profile and notifications
    • Name, email, WhatsApp number.
    • Preferred reminder channel (Email or WhatsApp), email notifications on or off, rebook nudges (opt-in).
    • Change password, Sign out, Delete account.
Left out on purpose
  • Sign-in with a WhatsApp code and SMS fallback from the design board: both need paid messaging services. Email and Google sign-in stay.
  • The message inbox: the history and emails cover it for now.

Review moderation

Who: an ownerStarts from: admin → Reviews (badge shows how many wait)
Planned
  1. Only real visits can be reviewed
    • A review is tied to one completed appointment; one review per visit.
  2. Automatic flags before you read it
    • Contains a link, contains a phone number, mentions the address or directions (private location), bad language, 1–2 stars (suggests a private follow-up).
  3. Decide
    • Approve: published with “verified visit” and the cut name; the client sees “Review published on the site”.
    • Reply as Skee: a short reply shown under the review, signed Skee.
    • Reject: hidden and kept in the archive; can be approved later.
    • Delete: removed for good.
    • Approved reviews can be unpublished at any time.

WhatsApp messages

Who: visitors, clients and ownersHow: free click-to-chat links with a prefilled message
Planned
  1. From the website to Skee
    • Hero, service card (“Question about the Skin Fade”), contact band, after booking (“About booking SKB-4827”). Live today with one general message; the per-button messages come with the booking work.
  2. From Skee to a client
    • On every appointment in the admin page: Confirm, Reminder, Location, Running late, Review. Each opens WhatsApp with the client’s number and the message ready; Skee taps Send.
    • A daily “To notify” list shows who still needs a WhatsApp reminder.
  3. Why not automatic
    • Sending WhatsApp messages without a tap needs the paid WhatsApp Business API. The tap-to-send route is free.

Later: promotions, more barbers, waitlist

From the design boards, after the core booking system
Later
  1. Promotions
    • A discount on a cut or add-on for chosen dates, shown on the booking screens; at most one promotional message a month.
